An end-to-end multi-agent system designed for B2B sales workflows, covering lead preparation, outreach, follow-up, and review.

### What I built

- Designed 12 reusable expert Skills for ICP definition, company research, lead scoring, sales talk tracks, objection handling, and funnel review.
- Created a unified data model connecting customer research, scoring, outreach, follow-up, and review records across agents.
- Built an interactive end-to-end demo supporting prospect screening, customer insight, personalized outreach, sales task management, funnel analysis, and optimization suggestions.

The system turns complex sales work into structured, staged workflows while keeping context and customer state consistent across agents.
